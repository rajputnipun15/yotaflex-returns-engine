# Requirements overview

The [full 92-entry audit](final_requirements_audit.md) retains the recorded Phase 5
descriptions and classifications. Its counts are 61 implemented and verified,
5 implemented but not independently verified, 22 partial and 4 not implemented.
They are historical scoped assessments, not a claim that publication preparation
has independently verified every requirement.

The main engineering requirements are a coherent returns lifecycle; the ten
required entities and exact IDs; full counts of 30,000 orders, 5,000 requests and
8,000 lines; one seeded starter/full generator; source-derived quantities and
money; explicit history; five labelled negative categories with separate truth;
CSV/JSON/SQL exports; validation and reproducibility; and honest documentation.

The submitted package includes unchanged source, tests and config, a starter
export example, selected evidence, and one canonical full export archive. The
public prompt record is an edited account of actual requests. Original records
and additional local evidence are retained privately.

Outstanding interpretation and implementation limits include the two original-
purchase boundary variants not being persisted as labelled Phase 4 fixtures,
scenarios separated from the baseline, incomplete exchange replacement, unsupported
override authorization, no external payment integration, no stock ledger and no
independently evaluated anomaly detector. The final submission report records
the actual publication status separately.
